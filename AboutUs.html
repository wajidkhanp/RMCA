About Masjid Arkan

Faith, knowledge, and community.

A welcoming place for Muslims and neighbors across Phoenix to pray, learn, and serve together.

Our mission

A masjid for every generation.

We aim to create a peaceful, caring environment grounded in the Quran and the Sunnah.

Masjid Arkan exists to provide a secure and comfortable place where believers can gather to worship Allah—regardless of race, nationality, ethnicity, gender, or age.

We seek to serve and engage both Muslim and non-Muslim communities with compassion, dignity, and the values of the Holy Quran and the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Our vision

We aspire to share a clear understanding of Islamic teachings, inspire appreciation of Islamic values, and build understanding throughout the wider Arizona community.

What guides us

Living faith through service.

01

Worship

Provide a welcoming and accessible space for prayer, reflection, and spiritual growth.

02

Knowledge

Help people of all ages deepen their understanding of Islam and the Quran.

03

Connection

Strengthen compassion, mutual understanding, and care within our wider community.

Join us

More than a place to gather.

We believe a masjid is a home for worship, learning, service, and meaningful relationships.
